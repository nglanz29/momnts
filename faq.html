Questions about momnts

Everything about how momnts logs the games you attend in person — the ticket stubs, the stadium quest, the players you saw live, and what it costs. Still stuck? Open the app and start logging.

What is momnts?

momnts is a personal sports attendance tracker — a keepsake of every game you've attended in person. Each game you log becomes a commemorative ticket stub, and momnts tracks the stadiums you've visited and the players you've seen live across MLB, NFL, NBA, and NHL. It's a collection of the games you were actually there for, not a stats dashboard.

Is momnts free?

Yes. momnts is free to start — you can log games and build your collection of ticket stubs at no cost.

Which sports and leagues does momnts support?

momnts covers the four major North American pro leagues: MLB, NFL, NBA, and NHL — spanning all 124 pro stadiums across those leagues.

How do I track which stadiums I've been to?

Every game you log lights up its venue on a live U.S. map across all four leagues. You can see which of the 124 pro stadiums you've crossed off and which are still left to visit, and keep a bucket list of the ones you want to reach.

How does momnts know which players I saw live?

When you log a game, momnts pulls that game's box score and automatically builds your roster of players seen live — including the exact stat line each player put up the night you were in the stands.

What are Vault collector cards?

When you witness something historic — a no-hitter, a walk-off, an overtime winner — momnts mints it into a holographic collector card with a rarity tier, so the rare moments you were there for stand apart from everyday games.

Do I need to download an app?

No. momnts runs in your web browser at momntsapp.com — there's nothing to install from an app store. Just open the app and start logging.

Is my data private?

momnts is your personal sports diary — your logged games, stubs, and collection are yours. See the privacy page for full details on how your data is handled.
